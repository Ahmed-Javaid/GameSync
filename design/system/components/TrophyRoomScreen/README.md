The Achievements page, from the rail's trophy (design system version 33; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "a way to track platinum / 100% achievements"). Every game's achievements together: how far you are, your Zeniths, what's closest to one, what you unlocked lately, what's within reach, and every game. A game's 100% is its **Zenith** (Platinum until the owner renamed it, 3 Oct 2026), in red and gold since version 49 (platinum before).

- **The band at the top**: the average completion of the games you've started (as Steam reckons it) as a large ring; achievements unlocked in all; **the Zeniths monument** (version 40; the owner: "I want it to be bigger, take more space. It needs to look mighty, something thats monumental"): since version 49 the Zenith's banner (Everest on red; the owner, 5 Oct 2026: "What if we had flags. I want the color of zenith to be red. Like the main its silver bronze and gold? zenith will be red", "maybe try difficulty of mountains as the tiers", "Keep everest as zenith and K2 as second"), nearly the band's height, with gold light fanning out from behind it all round (versions 45 to 48: the Zenith badge), and since version 50 that light moves (the owner, 7 Oct 2026: "Animate the light rays"): its rays turn once a minute, a finer, fainter set between them turns the other way every 90 seconds so the light shimmers where they cross, and its glow breathes, all still with reduced motion, the count in big numerals in the Zenith's own colours, gold to red and "Latest:" over the game's name (`ZenithMonument`; with none yet the badge stands faint, without its light); the tiers you hold (Gold, Silver, Bronze); and the rarest you have, with its game. **The tiers' banners rise at its right** (version 35's cups since version 49; the owner: "trophy signs in it, emerging from the right"): the Zenith's Everest tallest at the back, Gold's K2 and Silver's Matterhorn either side, Bronze's Fuji in front, in a light of their own (`TrophyRise`). They rise into place one after another as the page opens and then stay still; with Windows' animation effects off they're simply there. The stats keep clear of them. Decoration only: a screen reader hears nothing of it.
- **Zeniths**: each game you've finished, its cover with the Zenith medal and the day it was earned, newest first. **Its title has the Zenith's banner** (version 49; before it version 45's badge, pointy mountains in front of a red sun in a ring, version 35's diamond, version 37's sword, version 38's flag and version 41's mountain with a gold star), and **its corner the Zenith's scene** (version 47, as the owner drew it: "Make it something like that"): a big red sun in rings of dots behind a tall pointy peak and a smaller one either side, rising faint from the card's bottom edge (`ZenithScene`; version 45 had a large faint badge there). None yet: "None yet." and the game that's closest.
- **Two rows the columns share** (version 37; the owner: "I really dont like this weird gap left by close to a Zenith and within reach"): Zeniths beside Recent unlocks, then Closest to a Zenith beside Within reach, each card filling its cell so the cards beside each other start and end together; Within reach's rows are as tall as Closest's.
- **Closest to a Zenith** ("The games you've started, by how far you are"): the games you've started that aren't finished, by how far you are, each with its bar and how many to go; a click opens its achievements.
- **Recent unlocks**: the last five across every game, newest first: the badge in its metal, the name, the game with how many players have it, and the day; one opens its game's achievements with it picked. There's no longer list: each game's own has every one.
- **Within reach** (version 35; the owner: "the weird blank space ahead of closest to platinum needs to be filled"): beside Closest to a Zenith, the five locked achievements most of Steam's players have, in the games you've started, never a hidden one: the locked badge, the name, its game and how many players have it. The easiest steps towards a Zenith; one opens its game's achievements with it picked. The two columns now end near each other.
- **Every game**: a table of each game with achievements here (cover and name, with the medal at the end of a Zenith's cell; its bar and percent, unlocked of total, its tiers, its last unlock), sortable by Game, Progress, Unlocked or Last unlocked, a second click reversing it; a row opens that game's achievements on this page, Back returning to it. One Tab stop with Up and Down, Enter opening (A11Y-02).
- **Rarity** is asked of Steam once a week, by itself. Asking again now is in Settings, Achievements, Rarity (version 55; the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "Refresh rarity shouldn't be on the main achievements page, but in settings"), not on this page.
- **Glossy: full glass** since version 35 (the owner: "the trophy room cards need a bit more transparency"), as Home and the library, over the last game played; Solid as before. A game open on it shows its own art.
- Only the games whose achievements GameSync tracks are here. **Since version 54 that's GameSync's own record** (the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "The achievement system needs to work even without steam"; the page's subtitle: "Every game's, kept by GameSync on every PC"): every game with an unlock GameSync has seen anywhere (Steam's files on any of your PCs, a copy's own record, which GameSync finds by itself, or one marked by hand for a copy that writes nothing down), and every game Steam on this PC tracks or whose copy's record GameSync has found, even at 0 (version 48 had Steam copies Steam had run here and copies whose record was in a folder added in Settings). A copy with nothing written down and nothing marked shows Steam's list at 0 on its own page, not here. A game left out in Settings, Achievements isn't counted anywhere on this page. A game whose achievements nobody has unlocked is listed in Every game with 0%, never in the shelves.
- **Built 3 Oct 2026** (the app's Achievements page), from Steam's own files on this PC; version 35's changes built the same day.
- **Your achievements on every PC** (version 54; the owner, 8 Oct 2026, yes to keeping them in the Drive folder): with Settings, Achievements, **Keep them in the cloud** on, this page is the same on every PC: GameSync's record is kept in its folder on Google Drive beside the saves, and an achievement unlocked on one PC counts on all, played there or not. A GameSync account (the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "These will later be tied to an account") can take it over later (ACH-08).
