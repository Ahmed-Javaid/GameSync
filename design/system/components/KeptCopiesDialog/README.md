Dialog for a game whose save folder holds copies kept by hand beside its live save (KAN-61): only the live save syncs, and each copy comes in as a named save, in one step. Bloodborne players keep "Before Orphan\SPRJ0005" beside the live "SPRJ0005"; without it, the whole folder would sync as one save, every copy in every version.

Provide `game`, `live` (`{path, saved, files, bytes}`: the live save as this PC has it), `items` (the copies, as ImportKeptSavesDialog's: `{id, name, saved, files, bytes, same?}`), `skipped`, `wholeBytes` (the whole folder), `mode` (`sync`, or `backup` from Back up now, New named save… or Import kept saves… on a game not syncing yet, KAN-63), `stage` (`looking`, `review`, `keeping`, `done`), `look` and `progress` (how far the look and the keeping are), `onSync(bring)`, `onWhole` and `onClose`.

- **Review**: the live save in a well, under "Syncs between your PCs" (or "Backed up, not synced between your PCs"), with its path, when it was saved, its files and size. Then **Bring the N copies beside it in as named saves**, ticked: the copies by name with when each was saved, its files and size, oldest first; one identical to another here says Same as that one and isn't named again. Unticked, they stay in their folders, not backed up, and Import kept saves… brings them in later.
- The foot counts what's kept (the live save and N named saves) and its size, and says the folders stay as they are, and that copies made there later aren't synced (New named save… keeps one in a step).
- What syncing is, said first (KAN-79; the owner asked "What exactly is syncing? Is it the same as backup?"): "Sync keeps every version, on this PC and in your Google Drive, and brings the newest to your other PCs" (or, backing up: "Back up keeps every version, on this PC and in your Google Drive; it isn't synced between your PCs").
- The primary says what it keeps, naming the live save's folder: **Sync SPRJ0005 + 36 named saves** (or Back up …; Sync SPRJ0005 alone when the copies are left out), its tooltip "Syncs the live save, SPRJ0005, between your PCs, and keeps each copy as a named save you can restore". **Sync the whole folder as one save** (it was Sync all of it instead) keeps the whole folder as one save, for a folder that isn't what it looks like; its tooltip says how big each version would be: the live save and every copy.
- **Looking** (`#looking`): as it opens, each copy's files are read to find the ones the same as another, a folder at a time; a `JobProgress` says so, with how many folders and how much is read, and Cancel (KAN-80: the owner saw only words, and nothing moved).
- **Keeping** (`#keeping`): from the moment the primary is pressed it's busy ("Syncing…", a spinner, the dots counting up), keeping its colour, and a `JobProgress` in the foot shows the copies coming in: how many, how much, how fast and how long is left. The choice can't change meanwhile. Cancel becomes Close: the job carries on after the dialog closes, and the game's saves show how far it is. It's all done on this PC, so it doesn't wait for Google Drive.
- **Done**: that the live save syncs now, and where the named saves are (Named saves, on every PC), and that the upload to Google Drive goes on beside whatever the person does next, shown on the game's saves.
- Opens from Sync these saves (a game's saves, its page's Saves card) when the scan found copies beside the live save, and from Back up now, New named save… and Import kept saves… on a game not syncing yet. A game's saves show the copies beside the live save under Where the saves are (GameSavesScreen `#bloodborne`).
