A choice of three or more, such as what happens to a game's settings files: the current choice on a small secondary button with a chevron, and a `Menu` of the choices under it with the current one checked.

Provide `value`, `options` (`{id, label}`) and `onChange`, and `label` for screen readers ("Settings files: Back up on this PC").

- Esc or a click elsewhere closes it; the arrow keys move through the choices, as in any `Menu`.
- Two choices that both matter are `PillTabs`; on or off is a `Switch`.
