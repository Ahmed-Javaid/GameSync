**Built in version 57** (9 Oct 2026), as drawn; What's in a folder also opens from the library's Add game folder and Scan a folder for games…, and from Settings' game folders, and what's left out stays out of the library at every later scan.

Showcase of first-run setup in five steps: Welcome, Your games, Choose saves, Where saves are kept, Backups and startup. Built only from the components above; the steps on the left are a `SettingsNav`, and finished steps take the `check` icon. Redrawn in version 56 after a friend's first try (8 Oct 2026: "clicked next next next and skipped the entire first launch. He didn't set a folder nor did he setup drive. The wording used was also ambiguous", "When games are detected I need to be more blatant about SAVES are detected not files", "at the start you should be asked to add game folder to get things started", "There is no option to collapse opened games").

- There's no side rail until setup is done. Each step has one primary button, and **no step moves on by itself being clicked through**: where a choice matters, the primary waits for it, and passing it by is a separate, worded button. Back is `ghost`. A step in the left column can be gone back to, not jumped ahead to. Nothing is copied until the last step, and the left column says so.
- **Welcome** (`#welcome`) says what GameSync is for before asking anything: your saves are your progress, the small files each game writes; GameSync finds them, keeps a copy every time you play and brings them to your other PCs; **it never copies or moves the games**. Three cards (It finds your saves, It keeps every version, Your other PCs get them), then one worked example side by side: a game's save (backed up: its path, 1 file, 11 MB, in `ok`) beside the game itself (never touched: its install folder, 25 GB). Get started.
- **Your games** (`#folders`) asks for game folders first, before any scan: what Steam, Epic and the EA app hold is found by themselves (a `Card` per store with its count); **Your game folders** is for games installed any other way (GOG, an installer, a zip). With none, the card is an empty state, "No game folder yet", saying what's lost without one, with **Add a game folder…** as the step's primary; the foot offers **Carry on without a folder** (secondary), and a note says someone who only uses Steam, Epic or the EA app needs none. A folder picked opens **What's in <folder>** (`#scan-folder`, below); once added (`#folders-added`) it's a `FolderList` row saying how many games were added and left out (Review… opens the same dialog), and the primary is **Find saves**.
- **What's in <folder>** (`#scan-folder`; the friend: "FL STUDIO, GitHub repo folder, cursor and other relevant items were added to library. When someone scans a folder, a small popup should show which games it has detected and then user can select deselect those games") is a wide dialog listing every program GameSync found in the folder, in two groups: **Games** (ticked), each with its cover, name, program path in `mono` and why it's taken for a game (in the save list, a game engine, named in its GOG record); and **Probably not games** (unticked), each with why (an app, by the publisher its program names; a code project, with a `.git` folder; a tool). The primary counts what's ticked (Add 5 games). The same dialog opens from the library's Scan a folder for games… and Add a game folder, and when a later scan finds something new in a folder, GameSync asks with it rather than adding it.
- **Choose saves** (`#saves`, `#scanning` while the scan runs, with Continue waiting) is titled with what was found, "Saves found for 43 of 46 games", and says these are save files, usually a few megabytes, not the games. Above the groups: how many games are ticked and how much their saves come to, and **Close every group**. Each group is a `Card` whose tick covers every game in it and whose heading opens and closes it (a chevron; `aria-expanded`), with its count, its saves' size and what happens to it: **Back up and sync** (open), **Synced by Steam or Epic already** (GameSync keeps every version too), **Probably online-only** (unticked), **Saves of games not installed**, and **No saves found yet** (a clock instead of a tick: watched the first time they're played). An open group lists its games under small headings (Game, Its saves are in, Size, Found by). Last, **A game missing?** with Add a game or folder (`AddGameDialog`, `#add-game`). The anti-cheat note sits beside Back and Continue.
- **Where saves are kept** (`#cloud`) is a real choice, not a step to skip: a `radiogroup` of three cards, nothing chosen at first, and Continue waits ("Choose where your saves are kept first"). **Google Drive** (Recommended) opens Sign in with Google once chosen, then says who's signed in and the Drive's space (`#cloud-drive`); **A folder every PC can reach** opens Choose a folder… (`#cloud-folder`); **Only on this PC, for now** says nothing leaves the PC and saves show **Not synced** until a cloud is connected from Home, with a warn line that a failed drive takes the backups with it and other PCs won't get them (`#cloud-local`); the primary then reads **Continue with this PC only**.
- **Backups and startup** (`#daily`, `#daily-time` with the time field open): Start GameSync when you sign in to Windows and Back up every game every day at a time, both on by default, each saying why in plain words; then **Your setup**: the games and their size, the game folders (or "None: only games from Steam, Epic and the EA app are found"), and where saves are kept, each with Change to go back to its step. The foot says both run as the person without admin rights and that the first backup runs in the background. **Start using GameSync** opens Home and offers the tour (`GuideScreen`, `#tour`).
