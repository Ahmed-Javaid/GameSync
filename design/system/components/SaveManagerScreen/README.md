Showcase of the console-style save manager: stats strip, selectable save table and live log. Tick rows and press Share selected, press Share all, or press the download icon to import a friend's zip. A game's name in the table opens its saves (see Game saves).

- The games that sync have their own table above every game's (KAN-49, built 1 Oct 2026; drawn in version 35); its rows are the ones ticked, with Select all in its heading and Share selected and Share all in its toolbar. Import saves is the download icon in the top bar, and a zip dropped on the window opens it too.
- **Each table has a heading** (version 35; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "it should be more clear what the top table is and what the bottom table is"): an icon, its name, its count and a line on what it holds. **Syncing between your PCs** (a cloud with a check, in `ok`): backed up here and in the cloud, the newest save brought down to your other PCs; tick games to share them. **Every game with saves on this PC** (the saves icon): everything the scan found, syncing or not; a game's row opens its saves, where Sync these saves starts one syncing. The console's command line stays in each pane, saying its sort.
- The stats strip: the games that sync; **Backups on this PC**, the space the backup folder really takes on its drive (files stored once and packed, as they are on disk), with the drive and its free space under it; every version kept; the last backup; and how many conflicts wait for you (Conflicts, Needs you until version 41). The table's Size is each game's history as its versions list it.
