The popup when you unlock an achievement while playing (ACH-09, design system version 35; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "as someone is playing a game they need to be able to see a popup and a nice sound to show they've unlocked an achievement").

`AchievementPopup`: provide `item` (the achievement: `name`, `icon`, `pct`, `hidden`), `game` and `progress` (unlocked of total with it); `kind: "zenith"` for a game's 100%.

- **GameSync's own small window**, over the game in a corner of the screen: top right by default, or the corner Settings → Achievements picks. It's a separate window that stays on top; it never takes focus, a click or a key, so the game carries on, and it never touches the game: no overlay drawn into it, no hook, nothing an anti-cheat could mind (the safety rules: watching only).
- **What it says**: "Achievement unlocked" in its metal ("Hidden achievement unlocked" for one its game kept secret, whose name shows now), its name, its tier's word with how rare it is ("Gold · Very rare · 2.7% of players"), and on the right how far the game is now, unlocked of total with a small bar in its metal. Rarity not known yet: no metal and no tier word, the game's name instead. A Zenith's popup is larger: since version 49 the Zenith's banner unfurling from its bar (Everest on red; platinum's medal before), "Zenith reached" in gold running to red, the game, and every one of its achievements, lit gold and red from behind.
- **Its look is fixed**: dark glass (`#0e1014` at 94%) with a white edge, in every theme and both surfaces, since it sits on a game, not on GameSync; text in `on-art`. The metal lights it from behind the badge.
- **Motion**: in from its side over 0.28s, still for 5 seconds (a Zenith 8), out over 0.22s; two unlocks at once take turns. With Windows' animation effects off it simply shows and goes.
- **Sound**: a short chime GameSync makes itself, no file from anywhere: Bronze two notes, Silver three, Gold three with a shimmer, and a Zenith its own rising run. It follows Windows' volume for GameSync; Settings → Achievements turns it off.
- **Exclusive fullscreen**: a game running in exclusive fullscreen can't have a window over it, so only the sound plays, and the unlocks wait for one notification when the game closes ("2 achievements in Risk of Rain 2").
- **How GameSync knows**: Steam rewrites a game's stats file in its `appcache\stats` folder the moment the game stores an unlock, and its `RunningAppID` says which game runs; GameSync reads them, within a second. Games Steam doesn't keep achievements for get no popup yet.
- **Try it**: Settings → Achievements → Try the popup (now, or in 10 seconds, to switch to a game first), or `gamesync achievements popup`.
