An achievement's icon in its metal, with the tier chips, the Zenith medal and the Zenith badge that go with it (design system version 33; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "aesthetically impressive", 100% tracked as a Zenith, hidden ones kept hidden).

`AchievementBadge`: provide `icon` (Steam's picture, kept on this PC), `name`, `state` and `tier`; `size` sm (40, rows), md (64, shelves) or lg (104, the spotlight and the details).

- **Tiers come from rarity**, the share of Steam's players who have it: **Gold** under 5%, **Silver** 5 to 20%, **Bronze** 20% or more. Until Steam's percentages are known it has no metal (a `line-200` edge). The metals are fixed tokens (`bronze`, `silver`, `gold` and their `-deep`), the same in every theme, and never a status: the tier's word is always beside its metal somewhere on the screen (a `TierChip`, the details), so it never means something by colour alone.
- **Gold glows**: a soft halo in its metal. Silver and Bronze don't.
- **Locked**: Steam's own grey icon, at half strength, with a small lock on its corner.
- **Hidden** (locked, and secret by its game's choice): no icon and no name: a striped tile with a "?". Its name and description show once it's unlocked, never before, anywhere in GameSync.

`TierChip`: a tier's metal dot and its word, or a count ("12 Gold"). The Zenith's dot runs orange to red, as Zenith's word does, with a warm glow, fixed in every theme like the metals (version 52; the owner, 7 Oct 2026: "have dot version for zenith, not the flag. Use a red orange gradient, similar to the text color right now"; version 49's was gold with red in the middle). It's Home's Zenith, under the tiers' dots in its achievements card (`AchievementsOverview` small); not yet, it's an empty ring (`is-not-yet`). `ZenithMedal`: a game's Zenith, its 100% (Platinum until the owner renamed it, 3 Oct 2026): since version 49 the Zenith's banner (`TierBanner`: Everest on red, the sun at its height, a laurel), standing in the medal's box with a warm glow under it; `unfurl` drops it open from its bar as it shows (the Zenith's moment, the popup), and reduced motion keeps it still. Not yet: the banner's outline, dashed, round the `zenith` icon. Versions 44 to 48 had the Zenith badge (below) with a sheen.

`ZenithBadge`, retired from the screens in version 49 (the owner, 5 Oct 2026: "What if we had flags. I want the color of zenith to be red. Like the main its silver bronze and gold? zenith will be red", "maybe try difficulty of mountains as the tiers", "Keep everest as zenith and K2 as second"; the Zenith's symbol is its banner since) and kept here as it was, since version 45 (the owner, 4 Oct 2026, of version 44's print: "IN the red badge, make it pointy mountains"; and of the Achievements page, which still had version 41's mountain and star: "you didn't make zenith changes here"): the Zenith's symbol everywhere, a print inside a ring: a misty blue-grey sky; a red sun with halftone rings of white dots round it, fading outward, and finer ones within it; a tall pointy peak in front of the sun, its left face lit and its right in shade, with faint facets; a pointy peak either side behind it and a jagged, hazy range behind those; dark pines either side, two in front of the peak's flanks; pale grass in front; then a dark teal ring with a fine pale line inside and a ring of dots round it all. It's the earned medal, the Zeniths monument, and the Zeniths card's mark beside its title. Its colours are fixed in every theme, like the metals. The pines and the grass are drawn once with seeded randomness (`ZENITH_ART`, made with the app's by `tools/design-system/zenith-art.js`), so the app draws the very same ones. From 48px it has everything; from 30px a simpler halo, coarser grass and no facets; below that the sun, the peaks and the pines in the ring. Its glow, centred on it, has the sun's red and the ring's teal. It followed version 41's mountain with a gold star (`ZenithPeak`, gone since version 45) and night-sky patch, version 42's matte (reverted) and version 44's print with a rounded summit and two figures on top. Version 45 kept the night-sky patch for Home's Zenith strip, and version 46 took the strip away (Home's card is the simpler one again), so every Zenith medal is this badge. Version 41's tier coins (`TierCoin`) went with that card.
