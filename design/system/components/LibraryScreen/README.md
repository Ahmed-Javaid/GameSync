Showcase of the game library, laid out like Steam's: every game by name in a list on the left, and the covers, or the page of the game picked, on the right. Built only from the components above; try the search, the sort, a right-click and a click on a game.

- **The list** (`GameList` in a 272px `Card` that runs the page's height): `SearchField` at the top ("Search your games"; Ctrl+F anywhere in the library, or the rail's Search, puts the cursor there; Esc clears it; Enter opens the first match); under it how many games the view holds and the sort, a small ghost button naming the order ("Recently played") that opens a `Menu`: Recently played, Name, Hours played, Recently added. Then **Favourites**, then the view's other games, both in that order. Up and Down move through the list, Enter opens the game.
- **A row** is the game's small cover and its name, with the status under the name only when the game needs you or is running; games not installed on this PC are dimmed. A click opens the game's page; a right-click (or the Menu key) opens Play, Add to favourites (or Remove), Hide from the library and Properties.
- **The covers**: the top bar with My games and a count, `PillTabs` for the view (All games; Installed, only what's installed on this PC; Local, games in their own folders started without a store's launcher; Needs you with its count; and Software and Hidden when there are any) and Add game. The view is kept per PC, so a library shown Installed opens Installed next time. Local says what it holds and offers Scan a folder for games…, which picks a folder (G:\, E:\Games) for GameSync to look in, now and at every scan; the games found there get Play. The view's games as `GameTile`s, favourites first under a FAVOURITES `overline` and the rest under ALL GAMES, in the list's order, as many to a row as fit at 150px or wider. The tab and the search apply to both sides; with nothing matching, the covers say so.
- **A game's page** takes the covers' place and the list stays, so the next game is one click away. Its Back button, the breadcrumb's My games, Esc, Alt+Left and the mouse's back button return to the covers as they were, or to the page that opened it (Home); reached from the rail's Game library, the page still open leads Back to the covers. Its More menu adds Change the art… (its Properties, on Art). A game found only by its saves, and from no store, offers Locate the game…: its program, picked in Windows' picker, marks it installed here, and Play starts it from there. See Game detail.
- **Favourites** are kept per PC, like hidden games: the star on a game's page or its right-click menu adds and removes one.
- The page is at Home's strength; with a game's page open, it takes that game's art and full glass, as Game detail does.
