Every achievement of a game, from its page's View all (design system version 33; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "a way to see all unlockable achievements; hidden achievements stay hidden until they're unlocked"). From the reference images' "Achievements in …" screen: the list on the left, what the one picked is on the right.

- **Where**: beside the library's list, as a game's page is, with Back and the breadcrumb (My games › the game › Achievements); from the Achievements page (the rail's trophy) its breadcrumb starts there instead. Esc, Alt+Left and the mouse's back button go back.
- **The header**: "Achievements in <game>", where they come from (since version 54 "Kept by GameSync", then where GameSync saw them: "seen in Steam on this PC and on LAPTOP", "seen in this copy's own record", "marked by you"; then rarity from Steam's players), and the game's `AchievementsOverview`: the ring, the tiers unlocked and its Zenith.
- **The list** (`AchievementRow`): tabs All, Unlocked, Locked and Hidden, each with its count; a search (names and descriptions, never a hidden one's); a sort: Recently unlocked (unlocked first, newest first, then locked by how many players have them), Rarest first, Most players first (what's within reach), Name. One Tab stop; Up and Down move through it and the details follow. An unlock GameSync didn't see on this PC itself says so beside its day: "· LAPTOP" for one unlocked on another PC, "· marked by you" for one marked by hand (version 54).
- **The details** of the one picked: its badge large in its metal, its name and description, when it was unlocked (day and time) or Not yet, **Seen** for an unlocked one (In Steam on this PC, In Steam on LAPTOP, In this copy's own record, Marked by you; version 54), how many players have it with a bar, its rarity's word and its tier, and for an unlocked one where it stands among yours ("Your 3rd rarest").
- **Hidden** ones, locked: "Hidden achievement" in the list and the details, and "Its name and what it asks stay secret until you unlock it." Their share of players shows; nothing else does, and the search never finds them. Unlocked, they're like any other.
- **Zenith** (Platinum until 3 Oct 2026): when every one is unlocked, the ring turns platinum and the header says Zenith and the day it was earned (`#zenith`).
- **Kept by GameSync** (version 54; the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "if the achievement system just parrots what steam records and does, thats not the point. The achievement system needs to work even without steam. It can get information about WHAT achievements are there to begin with, but after that it needs to work without it. Tracking by itself or something"). Steam's list names the achievements; what's unlocked is GameSync's own record, kept with its day whether Steam is there or not, and the same on every PC once it's kept in the cloud (Settings, Achievements). GameSync fills it from every place an unlock is written down: Steam's files on any of your PCs, and the records copies Steam doesn't run keep themselves, which it finds by itself. Once seen, an unlock is never lost: not when Steam is uninstalled, the account changes, the copy is deleted or the game reinstalled.
- **Marking by hand** (version 54; the owner: "Yes but only for games that write nothing down"): only for a copy that writes its unlocks nowhere GameSync can find (a copy Steam doesn't run, with no record found, or since version 55 with a record that has never held an unlock: the owner's Sons of the Forest keeps its stats but never its achievements; the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "If the marking exists we should be able to do it"). The list says so in a note, "This copy writes its unlocks nowhere GameSync can find, so mark each one as you earn it. GameSync looks for a record again each time you play it.", and opens on Locked (`#marking`). A locked one picked has **Mark as unlocked** (primary), unlocked today and marked by you, counting like any other, the Zenith included; one marked by you has **Unmark**, which takes it off on every PC. A hidden one stays secret: **Show it to mark it** shows its name and what it asks, for this look only, so the person can find the one they earned (`#marking-hidden`). A game whose unlocks something writes down has no marking: Steam's or the record's say. A mark made before a record turned up stays, marked by you, and what the record says joins it.
- **Why GameSync can't see them** (version 55; the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "have a little question mark on the achivement page for the game that when hovered it explains why the game is prolly not being picked up"): a copy whose achievements are marked by hand has a `help` icon (a ?) after the header's words. Under the pointer, and to a screen reader as its description, it says why GameSync can't see what's unlocked in it, from what GameSync found when it last looked (`#marking-why`), each with the file or folder it means:
  - **No list of achievements**: the copy's Steam files have a settings folder (`steam_settings`) with no list of the game's achievements in it, so it can't record any.
  - **A file never written to**: the copy made a file for its achievements when the game first started and hasn't written to it since, though the game was played since ("it isn't recording them"); not played since, it's "empty so far: nothing unlocked yet, or the copy doesn't record them".
  - **Stats but no achievements**: the copy keeps the game's stats while its file of achievements stays empty. On Steam, Steam itself unlocks the achievements stats earn; such a copy doesn't.
  - **Nothing found**: no file in a folder named by the game's Steam number in AppData, Documents, Saved Games, Public Documents or ProgramData, nor in its own folder. Some copies keep none; a folder kept anywhere else can be added in Settings, Achievements.
  - **Not looked yet**: GameSync looks a minute after it starts, and while the game plays.
  Each ends with what to do: mark the ones earned; GameSync looks again each time the game plays. Copies are never named by who made them, and GameSync never changes a game's files to make it record (the owner: fixes like that are theirs, "separate than gamesync").
- **Its sizes**: the details take about a third of the width (280 to 420px) and the list the rest; the tabs wrap onto a second line when Windows' text is large (A11Y-04). The header's art is the game's, soft and faint, coming in from the right.
- Opening it asks Steam for every icon it shows that isn't kept yet (never a hidden one's). Built 3 Oct 2026.
- Steam's percentages come once a week; until they're known, the list's percents and the tiers are left out and the sort by rarity says it waits for them.
