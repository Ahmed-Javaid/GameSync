A job under way, shown where its result will appear: what it's doing, a bar, how far it is, how fast and how long is left (KAN-80). The owner, 1 Oct 2026: "Whenever some processing is occurring, the user needs to see some way to know it's working and not crashed", and "if it's uploading then I need a progress bar that shows the upload progress and speed".

Provide `title` (what it's doing, as a verb: "Uploading to Google Drive", "Bringing the copies in", "Looking at the copies beside the live save"), `value` (0 to 100; none while how much there is isn't known yet), `detail` (how far, in `mono`: "42.1 of 113 MB · 412 of 1,108 files"), `speed` ("6.1 MB/s") and `left` ("about 12 s left"). `state` is `running` (the default), `paused`, `done` or `failed`, with `note` saying why it's paused ("Paused while you play Bloodborne GOTY") or how it ended. `boxed` sets it on a card of its own.

- **Running**: a spinner in `primary` beside the title, the speed (or the percentage) at the right, the bar, and under it how far and how long is left. A light sweeps along the fill, so it moves even while the bytes wait; with no `value` yet, a stripe slides along the track and the title's dots count up.
- **Speed and time left** are averaged over the last few seconds, so they don't flicker, and appear once there's enough to say (after about a second). Sizes are the saves' own, before compression, as everywhere else.
- **Paused** (`neutral`, the pause icon): an upload waits while a game that syncs is played, and says so. **Failed** (`warn`, the alert icon): what went wrong and what happens next ("Google Drive can't be reached. It tries again at 21:14; your saves are safe on this PC."). **Done** (`ok`, the check): one line saying what it did ("Uploaded 113 MB in 19 s"), for a few seconds, then it goes.
- **Where**: in a dialog, in its foot, beside its busy button (KeptCopiesDialog's keeping, Import, Share's zip); on a game's saves, boxed under the status, for its uploads and downloads (GameSavesScreen `#uploading`), and there too while a job its dialog started carries on after the dialog is closed. First run's scan and Scan a folder for games show theirs where their results will appear.
- **BusyLine** is the same for a wait with nothing to count: a spinner and its words, with dots that count up ("Bringing back “befo ludwig”").
- With Windows' animation effects off, nothing turns, sweeps or counts: the spinner and the dots stand still, a bar with no size yet fills faintly, and the numbers and words still change as the job goes.
