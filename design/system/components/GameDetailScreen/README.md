Showcase of a game's page: about the game, with a little about its saves; the save work itself is in the save manager (see Game saves). It opens in the library beside the list whenever a game is clicked: a tile, a row of the list, a card on Home. Built only from the components above; pick other games in the list to see the other states.

- **Back** (a small secondary `Button` with `arrowLeft`) and the breadcrumb (My games › the game) sit at the top left. Back returns to where you came from: the covers, Home, or the save manager; Esc, Alt+Left and the mouse's back button do the same.
- **The hero** shows only the art, the eyebrow (the store; for a loose copy, its folder and engine) and the game's logo or name. Play and the facts sit under it in the `PlayBar`: Play; Last played, Play time, Achievements (a game whose store tracks them) and Saves (its status); then the favourite star, Properties and More (Hide from the library, Open the game's folder, Open the save folder, Properties). When the game needs you, the status's own action is the primary and Play sits beside it; a Steam game not installed here offers Install through Steam, and any other game not installed here Locate the game…. While it runs, however it was started (GameSync, a store, a shortcut) and whether its saves sync or not, the primary reads Playing and Last played reads Playing now. More adds Change the art… (Properties, on Art: its own cover, banner and logo).
- **About**, from the game's Steam store page, fetched once with its art (a loose copy of a known game gets it through the save list's Steam ID): three lines of its description with Show more, then `Facts`: Developer, Publisher, Released, Genres, Engine; Store page opens it in the browser. A game added by hand says there's no store page.
- **Achievements** (version 33; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: "aesthetically impressive"): the game's `AchievementsOverview` (its ring, Gold, Silver and Bronze unlocked, and its Zenith, earned with its day or how many to go), the latest five unlocked as badges in their metals with their days, and the rarest you have in an `AchievementSpotlight`; View all opens the `AchievementsScreen`. A Zenith's ring runs gold to red and glows, its medal the Zenith's banner (version 49; platinum before; `#sekiro/ach`; `#ror2/ach` scrolls to the card). **The first time a game's 100% is seen** on this PC, here or on Home, its moment plays: the ring fills its last stretch, flashes and bursts with gold sparks, and the banner unfurls (`#sekiro/ach-moment`); after that it just glows.
- **Every game's page has the card** (version 35; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: Cuphead and Child of Light had none: "it should still have achievements on its game page. Let it say 0"). **Since version 54 what's unlocked is GameSync's own record** (the owner, 8 Oct 2026: "The achievement system needs to work even without steam. It can get information about WHAT achievements are there to begin with, but after that it needs to work without it"): Steam's list names them, and every unlock GameSync sees, anywhere, is kept with its day on every PC. A Steam game seen here says "43 of 58 unlocked" (no longer "on Steam"). A copy that isn't Steam's (a game in its own folder) whose own record GameSync found by itself counts like a Steam game's: "27 of 81 unlocked · this copy's own record", the overview, the latest unlocked, the rarest, and a line saying GameSync found its own record and keeps what it sees there while each one's name, icon and rarity are Steam's (`#wukong/ach-record`). With nothing written down yet, it shows "0 of 81 · nothing written down yet" and a line saying GameSync watches for a record the next time it's played and until there is one each can be marked as it's earned, with **Mark achievements…**, which opens every achievement on Locked (`#wukong/ach`); once some are marked, "5 of 81 unlocked · marked by you" (`#wukong/ach-marked`). A game whose achievements were unlocked on another PC shows them here too, "12 of 63 unlocked · on LAPTOP", played here or not (`#hollow/ach-laptop`); with none anywhere, a Steam game Steam on this PC has never run shows its list at 0, none unlocked yet (`#hollow/ach`). View all lists every one, locked, hidden ones secret. Steam's list comes with no key, by the game's app ID, kept on this PC. A game with no list says so in a line: GameSync names achievements from Steam's lists, and Steam has none for it (`#bloodborne/ach`). A game Steam lists none for (asked, and its list is empty: the owner's Child of Light, whose achievements are Ubisoft's) is drawn the same way in its own words: **0 achievements on Steam**, and "Steam lists no achievements for this game. A game with its own launcher, such as Ubisoft Connect, may keep them there; GameSync reads Steam's for now." (a Steam copy says only the first sentence; as built, version 36). A game left out of the achievements (Settings, Achievements) says so, with Count it again (`#core/ach-off`).
- **Saves**, a little, so you can jump to them: the status and its sentence; when it last backed up and on which PC, how much history there is, how many named saves; the save folder with Open; and one or two actions: Back up now and New named save… (NamedSaveDialog); Sync these saves and Choose files… for a game not syncing yet; the status's action when it needs you. Open in Saves goes to the game's saves in the save manager.
- **On this PC**: where it's installed, its size, how it starts and its launch options, with Open the folder and Properties (see Game properties). A game or folder added by hand (`AddGameDialog`) says how it syncs instead: when its program closes, or, with none, once its folder has been quiet for 5 minutes.
