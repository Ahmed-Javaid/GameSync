Row of pill tabs that switch a view in place.

Provide `items` (`{id, label, icon?, count?}`), `value`/`onChange` or let it hold its own state, and a `label` for the tab list. The selected tab fills with `secondary-soft`; a `count` shows as a small `secondary` chip, used only for Conflicts (Needs you until version 41; the owner, 4 Oct 2026: "Rename needs you to conflicts"). While something needs the person, Conflicts takes `tone: "warn"`: `warn-soft` fill and `warn` text with a caution mark in place of its icon, and the count in warn on the page's colour, selected or not (the owner, 3 Oct 2026). With nothing to see it's a plain pill with no count.

- Top bar of the launcher: My games, Recently played, Favourites, Conflicts. Home's tabs open the library on that view.
- Top bar of the game library: All games, Conflicts, and Software and Hidden when there are any; the view applies to the list beside the covers too.
- Save manager: Saves, Plan, Versions, Log. Share window: Choose saves, Share all.
- Settings: theme mode (Dark, Light, Match Windows), history to keep, the conflict default.
