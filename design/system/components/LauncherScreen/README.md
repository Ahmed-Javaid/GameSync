Showcase of the launcher home: rail, top bar, hero, and the Achievements, Jump back in and Activity cards. Built only from the components above; switch the theme to see it in every preset.

- **The hero** is the game playing now, however it was started (Play in GameSync, a store, a shortcut) and whether its saves sync or not: its status reads Playing now, the eyebrow says since when, and there's no Play while it runs (`#playing`). With nothing running, it's the last-played game, with Continue playing. A game played with no saves found yet says learn mode is watching where it saves, and that it shows the places when you quit (`#learning`, version 43).
- In Glossy, Home is full glass, as a game's page is, so its cards take the game's colour. When the hero changes to another game, its picture fades to the new one; while a game runs, the hero's glow and ring in Playing's green ease in, and out when it stops.
- **Achievements** (the owner, 3 Oct 2026; version 32, made richer in 33) takes the place Needs you had: the hero game's `AchievementsOverview`, small: its 76px ring with unlocked of total under it (version 35 moved the count out of the ring, where it ran into it), its tiers in a column, and under them its Zenith, earned (the Zenith's dot, orange to red, and "Zenith" in gold to red, its ring glowing gold to red, `#zenith`; the first time it's seen, its moment plays and the dot pops in, `#zenith-moment`) or how many to go beside an empty ring. Since version 52 it's the Zenith's dot in the tiers' column, its words where their counts start, not the banner (the owner, 7 Oct 2026: "have dot version for zenith, not the flag. Use a red orange gradient, similar to the text color right now"); a game's page keeps the banner; then the last two unlocked, badges in their metals, each with its name and the day; its arrow opens the game's `AchievementsScreen`. Version 41 laid it out again (a 104px ring in the theme's two colours, the tiers as coins, the Zenith as a night-sky strip, the last three unlocked with their tier and rarity); the owner preferred the simpler card (4 Oct 2026: "The home achievement card should be the one before that. Without the zenith card. Where it was more simple"), so since version 46 it's this one again. When the hero game keeps none (a copy in its own folder, a game whose store doesn't track them) it's the last game played that has some, saying so in plain words ("Risk of Rain 2, played 1 Oct. Bloodborne GOTY has none GameSync can read.", `#noach`); with none at all, a line saying achievements show here for the Steam games you play.
- **Conflicts** (Needs you until version 41; the owner, 4 Oct 2026: "Rename needs you to conflicts") lives in the save manager (its Conflicts tab). While something needs the person, the top bar's Conflicts pill is in the warn colours with a caution mark and the count, and the rail's Save manager has a caution mark and its icon in warn, on every page ("2 conflicts"); the pill opens the save manager's Conflicts tab. With nothing to see, both are plain (`#fine`).
- The rail's Game library shows the `play` dot while a game runs, saying which ("Ghost of Tsushima is running"), and the running game's tile carries Playing wherever it shows.
- **An update ready** (version 53, `#update`): when a newer GameSync is downloaded and has passed GameSync's own signature check, the top bar shows **Restart to update** (a small secondary button with the download mark), its tooltip naming the version and what happens: GameSync closes, installs it and opens again in a few seconds. It waits while a game that syncs is running, saying why. Settings → Updates has the same, with what's new.
- The top bar's cloud button says where the saves go and whether it's reachable. When first run skipped the cloud, it's a small secondary button, Connect the cloud (`#nocloud`), which opens the Connect cloud dialog; until then every version stays on this PC.
- GameSync notices a game running by its programs, in the folder it's installed in, with the least access Windows gives (as Task Manager does); nothing touches the game itself. Its play is recorded, so play time and the activity calendar count it.

**The banner moves between the games played last** (version 37; the owner: "I want the main screens hero card to be scrollable, but I can't think of what it will scroll between. Recently played games?"): the game playing now or played last first, then the next four played, each with its art, words, status and Play. A glass pager sits at its top right: the game before, a dot a game (the shown one white; each named for screen readers, "Terraria, 2 of 5"), and the next. The wheel over the banner, a two-finger swipe, and Left and Right with it focused move between them too, round from the last to the first. It never moves by itself: a launcher opened to carry on playing shouldn't change under you. The Achievements card shows the shown game's, and Glossy's colours follow it, the light coming through from the click as when a game's page opens. A refresh keeps the game shown unless another game starts playing, which then shows. **A click on the banner opens the game's page** (version 37; the owner: "if a user clicks on the hero card, shouldn't they be taken to that games page?"), anywhere but its buttons and the pager, and so does Enter with it focused; it shows the hand and a focus ring.
