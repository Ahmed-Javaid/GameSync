A ring of progress: how much of a game's achievements are unlocked, around its percent. From the reference images' Overview ring (design system version 33).

Provide `value` (0 to 100); `label` goes in the middle (the percent by default) and `sub` under it ("123 / 171"). `size` defaults to 96 and the stroke to about a twelfth of it.

- The arc is `primary` on a `bg-400` track, with round ends; it draws in from the top over 0.9 s, and with reduced motion it's simply there.
- `tone: "zenith"` is a game's 100%, its Zenith (version 49; the owner, 5 Oct 2026: "I really want players to feel awarded when they hit 100%", then "gold and red seems good"): the ring runs from gold at the top to deep red at the bottom and back; a warm gold glow breathes round it (four seconds each way), a glint of light runs round it every six seconds, and faint sun rays turn behind it once a minute. Before version 49 it was platinum with a soft glow.
- `moment` plays once, the first time a game's 100% is seen (the app remembers it per game on each PC): the ring fills its last stretch, flashes, light bursts out with gold sparks, and then its light comes up; the Zenith banner beside it unfurls, or on Home's card the Zenith's dot pops in (version 52) (`AchievementsOverview`). Reduced motion: the ring and its glow simply show, still.
- **Seen means on screen** (found while building version 49, 6 Oct 2026): the moment waits until the whole ring is in view, so a card below the fold plays it when it's scrolled to, never out of sight. Its burst and sparks spill past the ring's card, over the page, and the rays and glow stay within it (the rays reach about a fifth of the ring past it). In the app the burst is drawn above the page while it plays, as a page's scrolling would cut it off at the card's edge.
- **Under 96px the count goes under the ring** (version 35; the owner, 3 Oct 2026: Home's "123 / 171" was spilling into the ring): the percent alone in the middle, larger, and unlocked of total under the ring, outside it, in `ink-faint` mono. From 96px both fit inside. `subBelow` overrides it either way.
- Always give it words: its `ariaLabel` says the numbers ("123 of 171 achievements unlocked, 72%").
